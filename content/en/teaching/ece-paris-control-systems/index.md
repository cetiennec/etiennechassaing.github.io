## Summary

From April to June 2026, control systems course at ECE Paris, taught to the full cohort of 500 students.

I taught the **second half of the course** — sessions 5 to 9, covering digital control:
$z$-transform, discretisation, sampled PID, filtering and Kalman estimation — along with
its assessments. The first half, on continuous-time systems, was taught by another
lecturer.

## Course material

All the documents handed out to students are published on this site — six lecture decks,
the final exam and a sample individualised quiz paper:

**[→ Course material and exams](/book/classes/ece/)**

The final exam is built as a single running case study on the altitude control of an
observation drone, and is [presented in full here](/book/classes/ece/examen-final/).

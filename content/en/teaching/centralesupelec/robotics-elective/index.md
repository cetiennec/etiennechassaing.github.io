I designed the CentraleSupélec elective **Robotics Systems, from Motion to Embodied AI**,
taught from April 2027.

It covers kinematics, dynamic modelling, trajectory planning and torque control; then
biomimetic versus learned locomotion, learning and formal guarantees; and ends with a
Python project in MuJoCo.

The course material will be published here once the course has started.
